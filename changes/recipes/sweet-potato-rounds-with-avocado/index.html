Sweet potato rounds with avocado

The rounds keep well in the fridge, so the avocado topping is best added just before eating.

Quantities scale. Cooking times do not. Original weights may be raw; optional seasonings can appear in the method.

LET'S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

Here's how.

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the sweet potato into 1 cm rounds and brush them with the olive oil.

  2. 02

    Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned at the edges.

  3. 03

    Mash the avocado with the lemon juice and spoon it onto the warm rounds.

A little kitchen note

The rounds keep well in the fridge, so the avocado topping is best added just before eating.
